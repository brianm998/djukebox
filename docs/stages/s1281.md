# s1281: non-finite playback positions (task #1295)

NaN and infinite playback times no longer reach totals, the progress bar or
seeking.

- `AVDoghouseAudioPlayer.playingTrackPosition` is nil when the player's time is
  not numeric; setting a non-finite position is ignored. Seeks now keep
  fractions of a second instead of truncating to whole seconds.
- A saved `RuntimeState` with a NaN or infinite position loads as nil.
- `TrackFetcher` shows no progress bar for a non-finite position or duration, or
  a duration that is not above zero.
- `format(duration:)` and the "time left" label return "" rather than trap on a
  value that does not fit an `Int`.

## Verification

The `tests` check. The `AVDoghouseAudioPlayer` getter and setter have no unit
test, and nothing was tried against live audio.
